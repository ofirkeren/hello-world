## 1. Project overview

- **What it is:** An AI character, Miss Rosa Mae, fronting social media accounts (Instagram first; TikTok and YouTube later). The main format is short videos where she speaks to camera, possibly with carousels too.
- **Subject:** Health of body and mind: sleep, stress, gentle physical health, happiness and self-worth.
- **Brand promise:** *Feeling better in your body and your mind, one small habit at a time.*
- **How it makes money:** She sells her **own digital products from her own landing page**: 2 ebooks, each also produced as an audiobook in her voice, and possibly workbooks.
- **Social content's job:** Build trust and an audience, and send people to the landing page.

## 3. Target audience

**Proposed, confirm with the user:** adults aged roughly 30–55, mostly women, who are stressed and tired: busy working parents juggling work, kids, bills and a packed schedule, who sleep badly and feel worn out. Older viewers (50+) are a secondary audience, especially for sleep and gentle body care.

## 4. The character

### Identity

| Attribute | Decision |
|---|---|
| Name | **Miss Rosa Mae** (working name; "Miss" signals respect and Southern manners) |
| Age | **67**: a well-kept 67, active, healthy and vibrant, never frail |
| Identity | Black Southern woman |
| Body | Full-figured |
| Accent | A soft, slow, rich Southern accent |
| Personality | Warm but no-nonsense. Teases with love, tells the truth, laughs at herself. Wise without being preachy. Dignified, with her own life, opinions and humor. |
| Role toward the viewer | A wise elder and mother figure, **not** a caretaker or servant |

### Backstory

- Raised in rural Georgia; now lives outside Atlanta.
- Retired after 30 years as a church secretary.
- Raised three kids.
- Cared for her own mother through old age.
- **Turning point at 45:** she hit a wall: no sleep, constant stress, worn out. Doctors told her to "rest more," but nobody told her *how*. So she started reading everything she could, testing it on herself and keeping what worked.
- 20+ years of self-teaching since then. She's a lifelong learner who is still reading at 67.

### Where her knowledge comes from

- **Self-taught:** decades of reading books and research.
- **Tested on herself:** she tried things and kept what worked and dropped what didn't. Lessons from raising three kids also shape her advice.
- **She is not a doctor or a nurse**, and says so openly from time to time.

## 6. Voice and language

### Voice
- Slow, rich, natural Southern accent. Calm and soothing: the kind of voice you could fall asleep to. This matters most for the audiobooks.
- **TBD:** ElevenLabs voice choice and settings (follow the Walter voice workflow).

### Language rules
- **Mostly standard grammar.** The accent in her voice carries the Southern flavor, not the spelling.
- **A light touch of Southern warmth:**
  - Terms of address: "baby," "sugar," "honey," "child."
  - Expressions: "Lord have mercy," "bless your heart," "I'm fixin' to tell you something," "now listen here." Use them sparingly and rotate them; don't repeat the same one in every video.
  - Plain, homespun wisdom, e.g. "You can't pour from an empty cup, and baby, yours has been empty a long time."
- **Avoid:**
  - Exaggerated dialect grammar (e.g. "you be tired," "she been knew").
  - Phonetic spellings in captions or scripts ("dat," "chile" everywhere).
  - **"Aks" instead of "ask."** Always "ask."
  - Any pronunciation or grammar feature commonly used to mock Black speech.
  - Slang in every sentence. A sprinkle feels real; constant slang feels like a costume.
- **Example intro:** "Well hey, baby, it's Miss Rosa Mae."
- **Example limits line:** "I'm not a doctor, baby. I'm just a woman who's done a lot of reading and a lot of living. If something's really wrong, you go see somebody."
- **Authenticity review:** a Black Southern woman (hired on Upwork or Fiverr) reviews voice samples and early scripts. Her judgment on what sounds natural wins. **TBD:** who, and how often.

## 7. Content: the 4 pillars

### Pillar 1: Sleep and Rest
- Why you wake up at 3 a.m. and can't fall back asleep
- An evening wind-down routine that actually works
- Phones in bed: what screens do to your rest
- Caffeine timing: the afternoon cup that steals your night
- Napping right versus napping wrong
- A cool, dark, quiet bedroom: small changes, big difference
- Racing thoughts at bedtime and how to quiet them
- Sleeping well after 50: what changes and what helps
- Weekend sleep-ins and why Monday feels so hard
- Rest isn't laziness: giving yourself permission to slow down

### Pillar 2: Stress and a Calm Mind
Stress throughout the day, in a stressful world: work, kids, bills, a busy life.
- Breathing to calm down in 60 seconds
- Being overwhelmed by work, kids and bills all at once
- Worry versus planning: knowing the difference
- How stress shows up in your body before your mind notices
- Saying no without guilt
- The morning rush: starting the day calm instead of in chaos
- Mindfulness for busy people, no meditation cushion required
- Letting go of what you can't control
- Doomscrolling and the news: protecting your peace
- Parents running on empty: refilling your own cup

### Pillar 3: Gentle Body Care
Specific, gentle habits from lived experience. **Not gym or coach advice.**
- A 10-minute walk after supper and what it does for you
- Water: why you're probably not drinking enough
- Sugar cravings and what they're really telling you
- Salt, swelling and feeling heavy
- Stretches for stiff mornings and achy backs
- Protecting your knees and balance as you age
- Sunlight in the morning and why it matters
- Sitting all day: small ways to move more
- Eating slower and actually tasting your food
- Listening to your body before it has to shout

### Pillar 4: Happiness and Self-Worth
Her gentle version of motivation and self-improvement. **No hustle or grind culture.**
- You don't have to fix your whole life by Monday
- Small habits beat big promises
- Speaking kinder to yourself
- Comparison: other people's highlight reels
- Gratitude that's real, not forced
- Forgiving yourself for lost time
- Finding joy in ordinary days
- Loneliness and staying connected
- Starting over at any age
- Being proud of how far you've come

**Post lighter versions on social media; keep the deeper, step-by-step versions for the books.**

## 8. Products

## 10. Guardrails (must follow)

1. **Label her as AI.** Use Instagram's AI label and say "AI character" in the bio. Put the same disclosure on the landing page and in each book's front matter.
2. **No fake credentials.** No "Dr.," no "nurse." She shares lessons, not medical authority.
3. **Real research behind every claim.** When she cites research ("researchers found…"), it must be a real, checkable study. Fact-check every script and every book chapter before publishing, and keep a source log (claim → source link).
4. **No disease or cure claims.** Keep to habits and general education. Say "talk to your doctor" whenever a topic touches on conditions, medications, or symptoms that could be serious.
5. **"Not medical advice" note** in each book and on the landing page.
6. **The kids:** frame lessons as raising them ("what I learned getting three restless kids to sleep"), never as testing things on them.
7. **No caricature.** Never portray her as the always-serving "mammy" figure; she has depth, opinions, humor and a life of her own. Don't call her "Mama" or "Auntie." Follow the language rules in section 6.
8. **Original content only.** If other accounts are studied for patterns, never copy their text.

## 11. Order of work

Follow the Walter sequence where it differs; this is the default:

1. **Read `WALTER-PLAYBOOK.md`** and set up the Rosa Mae project folder to mirror Walter's structure.
2. **Character sheet:** signature phrases, recurring segments, how she opens and closes each video. Build on sections 4–6.
3. **Visual reference set** in Higgsfield: face sheet, outfits, fixed scenes. Get user approval before mass production.
4. **Voice:** choose and tune the ElevenLabs voice; produce samples for the authenticity reviewer.
5. **Book 1 outline**, approved by the user, then the **Book 1 manuscript** (fact-checked, with a source log).
6. **Book 1 audiobook** and the optional workbook.
7. **Free lead magnet** taken from Book 1.
8. **Landing page** with checkout, delivery, email capture and the Book 2 waitlist.
9. **First batch of content:** 10–15 posts, mostly Pillars 1 and 2, scripted, fact-checked and reviewed. Then ManyChat flows and the Metricool schedule.
10. **Book 2** after Book 1 launches, shaped by audience response.
